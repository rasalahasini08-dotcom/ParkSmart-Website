⚡ Dynamic Surge Pricing Formula

Transparent mathematical algorithm utilized by ParkSmart across the 10 shared slots.

Occupancy Range Demand Classification Surge Multiplier Effective Hourly Rate Algorithm Action
0% to 49% Normal Flow 1.00x (Base) Base Rate (₹40 - ₹60/hr) Standard baseline hourly rate applied.
50% to 79% Moderate Congestion 1.25x (+25%) +25% Surge Factor Mild incentive to balance parking distribution across city hours.
80% to 100% Peak Congestion 1.50x (+50%) +50% Peak Surge Surge allocation rate activated to prioritize essential urban access.

Formula: Effective Price = Hub Base Rate × Dynamic Surge Multiplier. Calculated dynamically for each hub and time window based on real database records.
